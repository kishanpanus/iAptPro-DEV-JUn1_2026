Apt3M Features

Centralized Announcements

Send instant updates and notices to all residents with push notifications.

Maintenance Requests

Residents can raise and track complaints; admins manage and close issues easily.

Digital Payments

Pay maintenance bills securely with UPI and track payment history.

Gate Pass System

Manage visitor and delivery entries with QR-based approvals.

Polls & Voting

Gather community opinions quickly through in-app polls.

Marketplace

Buy and sell items within your apartment securely and commission-free.

Document Vault

Access bylaws, meeting minutes, and important society documents.
